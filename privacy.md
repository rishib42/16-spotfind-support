# Privacy Policy

SpotFind for Mac · Last updated October 18, 2026

SpotFind searches the files in the folders you choose, on your Mac. It is built so that your files and your searches never leave your computer.

## What SpotFind collects

Nothing. SpotFind does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## Your files and searches

To make files searchable, SpotFind reads the folders you choose and keeps an index of them inside its own app container on your Mac. All reading, indexing and searching happens on your Mac. Your files, the index and what you type into the search field are never uploaded or sent anywhere. SpotFind only reads your files; it never changes them.

Removing a folder in SpotFind's Settings removes it from the index. Deleting the app removes the index with it.

## Network use

SpotFind does its work offline. The only network traffic comes from Apple's App Store, which handles the subscription (see below).

## Subscription and payments

The subscription is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. SpotFind never sees your payment details. It only asks the App Store whether your subscription is active. Apple's handling of that information is described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

SpotFind does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
